08 / Questions

Straight
answers.

The useful details, before we begin.

Do you provide a booking platform?

The core project is a bespoke property website with clear inquiry paths and links to your existing booking service. A custom booking tool can be scoped separately from The Immersive tier onward.

Do you host the website?

Hosting and domain registration are separate. We prepare and hand over the completed site for the provider you choose, with the files and access documented in the proposal.

How long will a project take?

Timing depends on the scope and the content available. After learning about your property, we give you a proposal with milestones, deliverables, and a realistic launch date.

Can we work together from overseas?

Yes. Discovery, feedback, and handover can happen remotely. If new photography, drone work, or an on-site visit is needed, we plan that with you as a separate part of the scope.

What if my property is still being built?

Plans and reference material can guide the story and an illustrative 3D model while the property is in progress. Any guest-facing dimensions or claims should be verified before launch.

What does the introductory price include?

The Essential starts at $1,490 instead of $1,990 and includes a custom-designed website, responsive layouts, editorial galleries, your property story, and inquiry connections. Final scope is agreed in your proposal.
